## 9. Advanced Patterns

### 9.1 Map-Reduce for Parallel Corner Simulations

In analog design, the circuit must meet specifications across **process corners**: TT (typical-typical), FF (fast-fast), SS (slow-slow), SF (slow-fast), FS (fast-slow). These simulations are independent and can run in parallel:

$$
\text{results} = \bigoplus_{c \in \{\text{TT, FF, SS, SF, FS}\}} \text{simulate}(c, \text{netlist})
$$

where $\bigoplus$ denotes the reduce (aggregation) step.

### 9.2 Fan-Out / Fan-In for Multi-Topology Exploration

When the optimal topology is unknown, we can **fan out** to explore multiple candidates in parallel:

$$
\text{topologies} = \{\text{telescopic}, \text{folded-cascode}, \text{two-stage}\}
$$

Each is sized and simulated independently, then the results are **fanned in** and the best is selected.

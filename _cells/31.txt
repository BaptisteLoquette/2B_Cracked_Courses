## 8. Subgraph Composition

### 8.1 Modular EDA Flows

Complex SoC design flows naturally decompose into subgraphs:

$$
G_{\text{SoC}} = G_{\text{analog\_FE}} \;\circ\; G_{\text{digital\_BE}} \;\circ\; G_{\text{integration}}
$$

Each subgraph is an independent `StateGraph` that can be:
- Developed and tested in isolation.
- Reused across different chip projects.
- Composed into a parent graph with clear interface boundaries.

### 8.2 Interface Contract

For subgraph $G_i$ with state schema $S_i$, the parent graph $G_{\text{top}}$ wraps each subgraph in an **adapter node** that maps between the top-level state $S_{\text{top}}$ and the subgraph state $S_i$:

$$
n_{\text{adapter},i}(s_{\text{top}}) = \text{extract}(s_{\text{top}}) \xrightarrow{G_i} s_i \xrightarrow{\text{inject}} s_{\text{top}}
$$

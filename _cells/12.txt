## 3. Building an EDA Workflow Graph

### 3.1 Defining the EDA State

The state captures the full snapshot of a design iteration. Every field is typed, enabling static analysis and IDE auto-completion.

Key PPA (Power-Performance-Area) metrics we track:

$$
\text{FoM}_{\text{OTA}} = \frac{A_{v,\text{DC}} \cdot f_{\text{UGF}}}{P_{\text{diss}}}
$$

$$
\text{PM} = 180° + \angle H(j\omega_{\text{UGF}})
$$

where $A_{v,\text{DC}}$ is DC gain, $f_{\text{UGF}}$ is unity-gain frequency, $P_{\text{diss}}$ is power dissipation, and PM is phase margin.
